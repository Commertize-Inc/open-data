# Commertize sponsor glossary: twelve real-world asset classes

*Version 0.1.0, 2026-10-20. License CC-BY-4.0. Machine-readable: `asset_classes.json`.*

**Tokenize** — To tokenize an asset is to issue interests in, or backed by, the asset or the entity that owns it, and to keep the ownership register for those interests on a blockchain as tokens, with transfer restrictions built into the token.

Accepting sponsor inquiries means Commertize will review a sponsor's materials for this class. It is not a statement that any asset in the class has been tokenized or will be on any timeline.

## Commercial real estate (c01)

**Status:** accepting sponsor inquiries

**What it is.** Income-producing property: single-tenant net lease, industrial, multifamily, self-storage, hospitality and healthcare property companies.

**What tokenizing it means.** The LLC that already holds title issues a new investor class, and that class's register is kept on chain as tokens. Distributions follow the operating agreement.

**What the sponsor supplies:**
- Deed and title report
- Operating agreement and member list
- Rent roll and leases (or lease abstracts)
- Trailing-12-month operating statement
- Current appraisal
- Existing loan documents and lender acknowledgment of the new class

**Terms you will see:** net lease, cap rate, DSCR, rent roll, estoppel, due-on-transfer

## Private-company equity (c02)

**Status:** accepting sponsor inquiries

**What it is.** Shares or units of an operating private company.

**What tokenizing it means.** The company issues a new class (often non-voting preferred or units) or converts its existing cap table to an on-chain register.

**What the sponsor supplies:**
- Charter and bylaws or operating agreement
- Current cap table
- Financial statements
- Most recent valuation (priced round or 409A)
- Transfer restrictions, ROFR and drag-along terms

**Terms you will see:** cap table, preferred, ROFR, 409A, holder of record

## Private credit and receivables (c03)

**Status:** accepting sponsor inquiries

**What it is.** Loans and contracted receivables that pay a defined stream.

**What tokenizing it means.** An existing credit fund issues a new class or series of notes, or a purchaser entity buys the receivables and issues notes backed by them.

**What the sponsor supplies:**
- Loan tape or receivables schedule
- Underlying contracts (checked for assignability)
- UCC-1 search results
- Servicing agreement
- Payment history

**Terms you will see:** receivables, true sale, UCC-1, assignability, waterfall

## Oil and gas royalties and minerals (c04)

**Status:** accepting sponsor inquiries

**What it is.** Mineral and royalty interests that receive a share of production revenue.

**What tokenizing it means.** The existing mineral or royalty LLC issues a new class, so royalty deeds and division orders stay where they are.

**What the sponsor supplies:**
- Title chain and royalty deeds
- Division orders and operator pay decks
- Production history from state records
- Revenue statements

**Terms you will see:** royalty interest, division order, decline curve, net revenue interest

## Energy infrastructure: solar, storage and hydro (c05)

**Status:** accepting sponsor inquiries

**What it is.** Operating generation and storage projects with contracted revenue.

**What tokenizing it means.** The sponsor's holding company or project company issues an investor class that receives project cash flow.

**What the sponsor supplies:**
- Power purchase or capacity agreements
- Interconnection agreement
- Metering data separating generation and capacity
- Tax-equity documents and consents
- Operating history

**Terms you will see:** PPA, interconnection, capacity, tax equity, metering

## Data centers (c06)

**Status:** accepting sponsor inquiries

**What it is.** Powered shell or turnkey facilities under a credit lease, and operating colocation.

**What tokenizing it means.** The title-holding LLC issues an investor class; for a levered operator, a holding company above the lender's change-of-control perimeter issues non-voting units.

**What the sponsor supplies:**
- Lease and tenant credit information
- Power and site-control documents
- Credit agreement and lender consent
- Operating statements

**Terms you will see:** powered shell, colocation, site control, change of control

## GPU and compute (c07)

**Status:** accepting sponsor inquiries

**What it is.** Compute equipment and the contracted capacity revenue it earns.

**What tokenizing it means.** The machine-owning entity or a holding company above it issues units that receive lease or capacity revenue, with lender consent and estoppel obtained first where the equipment is financed.

**What the sponsor supplies:**
- Equipment schedule and serial numbers
- Offtake or capacity contracts
- Lender consent and estoppel
- Utilization and revenue history

**Terms you will see:** offtake, estoppel, utilization, capacity revenue

## Farmland (c08)

**Status:** accepting sponsor inquiries

**What it is.** Agricultural land and its lease or crop revenue.

**What tokenizing it means.** An existing farm LLC issues a new investor class, after a check that state farm-ownership rules allow it.

**What the sponsor supplies:**
- Deed and title
- Farm leases
- Yield and revenue history
- Water and access rights documentation

**Terms you will see:** cash rent, crop share, water rights

## Sports venue receivables (c09)

**Status:** accepting sponsor inquiries

**What it is.** Contracted, assignable venue revenue such as premium seating, naming rights or concessions contracts.

**What tokenizing it means.** A purchaser entity holds the receivable and issues notes backed by it; league and counterparty consents come first.

**What the sponsor supplies:**
- Underlying revenue contracts
- Consent from the league and counterparties
- Existing bond-indenture pledges on the same revenue
- Payment history

**Terms you will see:** receivable, anti-assignment, lockbox

## Music and IP royalties (c10)

**Status:** not offered at this time

**What it is.** Royalty income from music catalogs and other intellectual property.

**What tokenizing it means.** A catalog or publishing company issues a class that tracks royalty income.

**What the sponsor supplies:**
- Chain of title
- Royalty statements
- Termination-rights analysis

**Terms you will see:** catalog, publishing, chain of title

## Producing mines (c11)

**Status:** accepting sponsor inquiries

**What it is.** Operating mines and royalty or streaming interests in their output.

**What tokenizing it means.** The operating company or an existing royalty holder issues a new class.

**What the sponsor supplies:**
- Technical report (S-K 1300 or NI 43-101)
- Permits
- Production and revenue history
- Reclamation obligations

**Terms you will see:** NSR royalty, S-K 1300, offtake

## Vaulted collectibles (c12)

**Status:** not offered at this time

**What it is.** Art, watches, wine and other collectibles held in a vault.

**What tokenizing it means.** A title token represents one specific item held in custody.

**What the sponsor supplies:**
- Provenance
- Independent appraisal
- Custody agreement

**Terms you will see:** provenance, custody, title token
